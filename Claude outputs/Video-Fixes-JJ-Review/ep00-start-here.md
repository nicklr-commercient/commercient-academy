# Episode 0 · Start Here: Your First Commercient Project

**Series:** Commercient Partner Implementation Series (see the series bible)
**Stage:** Orientation
**Watch this:** before your first project starts.

This episode doesn't use the nine-beat meeting structure. It's an orientation: what this series is, how you'll learn, and how to use these videos. **Don't explain what Commercient is or why companies connect their ERP and CRM.** The viewer already knows Commercient and sells it. Start with the training, not the product.

---

## Beat 1 · What this is

This is an orientation and training-prep series. You'll learn to implement Commercient **live, alongside the Commercient team,** on a real customer project. These short videos don't replace that. They support it: one video before each meeting, so you walk in knowing what the meeting is for, what will happen, and what it should produce.

## Beat 2 · How you'll learn: the three-customer ramp

1. **Customer 1: you shadow.** Commercient's team runs every meeting. You attend all of them, prepare for each one, and take on small pieces as you go.
2. **Customer 2: you lead.** You run the meetings. Commercient supports you at every step.
3. **Customer 3: you run it.** Commercient is there when you need it.

This series prepares you for **Customer 1.** Every meeting has a named Commercient lead who guides you through it. A person, not a packet.

## Beat 3 · The map: 14 meetings

A project has three stages: **Installation** (3 meetings), **Phase 1** (5 meetings) and **Phase 2** (6 meetings, when the customer buys it). Show the full series map, empty, then light up the three stages one at a time.

Installation always comes first. **Phase 1 and Phase 2 can overlap:** once the first sync is verified and working, Phase 2 can run alongside Phase 1, if the customer bought it and agrees. The series covers them in order to keep things simple.

The one idea to carry through the series: **every meeting produces something the next meeting depends on.** Each of the next 14 episodes covers one meeting, in order.

## Beat 4 · Before your first customer meeting

You can't join customer meetings or get system logins until three things are done:

1. **Complete CGI onboarding.**
2. **Sign the NDA.**
3. **Sign a TPAF**, if the project requires one.

Commercient's partner team walks you through all three. Finish them before Customer 1 starts.

## Beat 5 · How to use these videos: your prep hour

Before every meeting, plan on about **one hour**:

1. **Watch the episode** for that meeting.
2. **Read that meeting's section** of the partner prep guide.
3. **Do one hands-on task**, practicing on a test system where one is available.
4. **Bring one question** to the meeting.

## Beat 6 · Your team

Mirror Commercient's team: a **delivery lead** for every meeting, plus an **installer**, a **Phase 1 configurator**, a **data matching specialist** and a **Phase 2 configurator.** One person can cover several roles. The configuration roles need real SQL skills.

## Beat 7 · Meet Cobalt Creek Supply

The example customer we'll follow through every episode. Say clearly it's **fictional.** A wholesale distributor on **Acumatica** (cloud) and **HubSpot**, with companies and contacts already in HubSpot. They bought Phase 1 and Phase 2 (new HubSpot companies become Acumatica customers; HubSpot deals become sales orders).
