## Where we are

Episode 3. Meeting I3: Record Counts and the Handoff. Installation, stop three of three, the last installation meeting. Show the series map with I1 and I2 filled in and I3 highlighted.

## Previously

In Episode 2, Cobalt Creek's Acumatica was connected by API, Level 2 finished the setup, and the installation team checked the record counts. Because HubSpot already has Cobalt Creek's data, the sync into HubSpot stays off for now.

## Why this meeting exists

To get the customer's **approval of the record counts**, give them a first look at their ERP data as Commercient sees it, and capture what they want changed. Then hand the project to the Phase 1 team. This meeting can also run as the opening of the Phase 1 kickoff.

## Who's in the room

- The customer's **project owner** and **CRM admin**
- Commercient's **installation lead**, with the **Phase 1 team** joining for the handoff
- **You**, shadowing

## What happens

1. **Approve the record counts.** The installation team shows how many records of each object Commercient sees in the ERP: customers, contacts, items, orders. The customer confirms the numbers look right. Nothing moves forward until they approve.
2. **Check whether the CRM already has data.** This decides what happens next:
   - **CRM already has data:** the sync into the CRM stays off. Existing CRM records get **matched** to the ERP first (Episode 5), so nothing is duplicated.
   - **CRM is empty:** a small batch of **sample records** can go to the CRM now for review.
3. **Look at the data.** Walk through a few real records, field by field, against the ERP screen. When the CRM sync is still off, review them in Commercient's portal.
4. **Agree mapping adjustments.** Anything missing, wrong or unwanted goes on the **customization list**: extra fields, picklists (they aren't synced by default), and filters.
5. **Confirm the sync frequency** agreed in Episode 1, and how it shows up in the customer's monthly usage.
6. **Book the Phase 1 kickoff.** It covers layout training, data matching and the customization requirements.

## Your prep hour

1. **Watch this episode.**
2. **Check the record counts** against what the customer expects. If a number looks off, ask before the meeting.
3. **Draft a customization list** before the meeting, so the discussion starts from something concrete.
4. **Bring one question.**

## What you walk out with

- Your action items:
  - Send the customization list.
  - Hand your **install record** from Episode 2 to the Phase 1 lead.

**On your next project, you'll run this review.**

## Watch-outs

- **No approval, no next step.** Record counts are approved by the customer, not assumed.
- **Existing CRM data means the CRM sync waits.** Turning it on before matching creates duplicates.
- **Be specific.** Each item on the customization list names the object, the field and an example record.
- **Don't promise changes yet.** The Phase 1 team decides what's standard, what's self-serve and what's extra scope.

## Cobalt Creek in this episode
